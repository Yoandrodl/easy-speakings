# Privacy Policy for Easy Speakings

**Last Updated:** September 2026

Welcome to **Easy Speakings**. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect
The Easy Speakings application is designed to respect your privacy. 
- **Camera & Microphone Access:** Our app may request access to your device's camera and microphone exclusively for the purpose of the "Smart Teleprompter" and local self-evaluation features. **We do not record, store, or transmit any audio or video data to our servers.** All processing happens locally on your device.
- **Personal Data:** We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personal identification information (such as your name, email, or phone number) through the app.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
Any permissions requested (such as Camera) are used strictly in real-time to provide the core functionality of the application (e.g., acting as a digital mirror while you read the teleprompter). 

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our app does not use third-party analytics or advertising frameworks that collect user data.

## 4. Data Security
Because we do not collect or transmit your personal data or camera/microphone feeds, there is no risk of your sensitive data being intercepted or breached from our servers. 

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date of this document.
