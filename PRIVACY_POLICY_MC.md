# Privacy Policy for Kelas MC Bandung & Teks Acara

**Last Updated:** October 2026

Welcome to **Kelas MC Bandung & Teks Acara** (part of Easy Speakings Academy). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and usage.

## 1. Information We Collect
Kelas MC Bandung & Teks Acara is an educational and practical utility tool designed with complete respect for user privacy:
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not require account registration, login credentials, phone numbers, or email addresses to use the core features of the app.
- **Local Teleprompter Data:** Any custom scripts or text modifications you input into the Teleprompter are stored strictly locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your scripts on any external servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services
Our application does not include third-party data tracking, ad tracking SDKs, or analytics frameworks that capture identifying user data.

## 3. Data Security
Because no sensitive or personal information is transmitted to external servers, there is no risk of data breach or interception from our systems.

## 4. Updates to This Policy
We may periodically update this policy to reflect any feature additions. Any revisions will be reflected by the "Last Updated" date of this document.
